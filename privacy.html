Privacy / Block: Build It Up

Information handling, clearly stated.

Effective date: September 18, 2026

OperatorAquastar GamesProductBlock: Build It Up

This Privacy Policy explains what information is handled when you use Block: Build It Up, what remains on your device, and what is processed by integrated advertising providers. The game is operated by Aquastar Games (the “Service Provider”).

Local-first savesProgress and settings remain in the app’s private storage.
No player accountsNo chat, profiles or developer-run game servers.
Advertising partnersAd-related data is processed by third-party SDKs under their policies.

1. Scope and key points

  • The Application does not require an account and does not provide chat or social profiles.
  • Game progress and settings are stored locally and are removed when app data is cleared or the Application is uninstalled, subject to operating-system backups.
  • Advertising is delivered through Unity LevelPlay mediation. The current mediation stack includes Unity Ads and Yandex Mobile Ads; AppLovin may be added in a future update.

2. Information stored on your device

The game stores a local save.json file in the Application’s private storage (Application.persistentDataPath). It may contain story progress, endless-mode best score, coins, hearts, boosters, music, sound, haptics, language and tutorial settings.

  • Aquastar Games does not operate a server that receives this save file.
  • The Application itself does not request your name, email address, telephone number or precise location.
  • Deleting app data or uninstalling the Application removes the local data, subject to device and backup behaviour outside our control.

3. Advertising and automatically collected information

When advertising is displayed, the mediation SDK and participating ad networks may automatically process:

  • advertising identifiers, including the Google Advertising ID and Android AD_ID permission;
  • device and technical information, including model, operating-system version, app version, language and network state;
  • IP address, including for approximate location and fraud prevention by ad providers;
  • advertising and app events, including impressions, clicks, video completion, rewarded callbacks and SDK diagnostics.

This information is used by the relevant providers for ad delivery, frequency capping, fraud prevention and measurement. The Application uses INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE and AD_ID for these functions. VIBRATE supports haptics and is not used for data collection.

4. Third-party providers

Third-party providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies:

  • Google Play Services — Google Privacy Policy
  • Unity Ads / LevelPlay — Unity Privacy Policy
  • ironSource / Unity LevelPlay — ironSource Privacy Policy and LevelPlay documentation
  • Yandex Mobile Ads — Yandex Privacy Policy
  • AppLovin, if enabled in a future release — AppLovin Privacy Policy

The exact set of mediated networks may change as the Application is updated. The categories of information remain those described in this Policy.

5. Cookies, consent and use of information

The Application does not set browser cookies. Third-party advertising SDKs may use SDK-local identifiers and similar technologies for delivery, frequency capping and measurement. Where consent is required by law, the Application requests it through the applicable consent-management flow before personalised advertising is shown.

Aquastar Games uses the information under its control to operate the offline-first game, support advertising functionality, apply frequency limits, prevent fraud and diagnose advertising integrations. The Application has no push or email marketing programme.

6. Disclosure and international processing

Automatically collected advertising information may be disclosed to the providers listed above, where required by law, or where necessary to protect rights, safety and the integrity of the service. Aquastar Games does not sell personal information. Ad-network processing and any sale or sharing defined by those providers is governed by their policies and your consent or device settings.

Aquastar Games operates no server-side personal-data store for the Application. Third-party providers may process information outside your country, including outside the EEA, using the lawful mechanisms described in their policies.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Uninstalling the Application stops further collection by the Application. Information already sent to an ad provider remains subject to that provider’s retention policy.
  • You may request access, correction or deletion of personal data held by Aquastar Games, withdraw consent, or submit applicable privacy-rights requests by emailing aquastarsupport@gmail.com.
  • Device-level controls include Android Settings → Privacy → Ads → “Delete advertising ID”, together with the controls offered by Google, Yandex and AppLovin.

8. Retention

  • Local game data remains on your device until you delete it. Aquastar Games keeps no server copy.
  • Advertising providers retain identifiers and event data under their own policies; their retention periods may differ by provider and jurisdiction.
  • Information required for legal compliance is retained for the period required by applicable law.

9. Children

The Application is a general-audience casual puzzle game and is not directed at children under the applicable age of digital consent. Aquastar Games does not conduct behavioural profiling. Where required by law, personalised advertising to a user below the applicable threshold is shown only with verifiable parental consent; otherwise contextual or non-personalised advertising is used.

10. Security and policy changes

Aquastar Games applies reasonable safeguards to the code and configuration it controls. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure. If a breach affecting personal information under our control occurs, we will provide notice as required by law.

Material changes will be published with a new effective date. Where legally required, consent will be requested before the change takes effect. Previous versions are available from aquastarsupport@gmail.com.

11. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact aquastarsupport@gmail.com. The Service Provider is Aquastar Games.
